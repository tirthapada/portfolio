THE PROBLEM: URBAN HEAT ISLANDS
The Core Issue
Dense urban areas trap heat due to concreate surface, roads, and a lake of green cover, creating dangerous temperature spikes.
The Planning Gap
Urban planners lack localized, data –driven tools to test how much a new park, cool roof, or water body will lower the temperature before physical construction.
