DATA INGESTION
Single report, bulk Excel/CSV, PDFs & images
1,000 reports → processed as one evidence pool
LOCATION SOURCING
GPS, map-pin selection, text extraction, or CSV coordinates
AI fuses evidence per location — it does not assume events
HARDWARE REQUIREMENT
No mandatory hardware — software-first prototype
Future: IoT sensors, drones, satellite feeds, govt. systems
CHALLENGES & MITIGATION
Conflicting / duplicate reports → contradiction & embedding checks
Outdated info → recency weighting |  Scale → batch ingestion

IMPACT, INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATION
TEAM CHANDRA | THE POST-DISASTER INFORMATION FOG
D-FUSE:
Collect → Understand → Compare → Verify → Fuse → Predict → Prioritize → Recommend
1. Dynamic Evidence Fusion
Combines multiple independent sources instead of trusting one report
2. Explainable Confidence
Shows WHY a score is high: recency, confirmations, images, reliability
3. Temporal Intelligence
Detects Stable → Deteriorating → Critical trends over time
4. Uncertainty-Aware Priority
High risk + low confidence triggers “Verify this location first”
5. Dynamic Priority Ranking
Ranking updates automatically as new reports arrive
6. Resource-Aware Support
Recommends how limited resources should be distributed
